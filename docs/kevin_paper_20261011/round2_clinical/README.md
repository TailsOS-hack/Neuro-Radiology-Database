# Exploratory patient-level clinical AD/CN experiment

This is a separate endpoint and model. Source clinical AD/CN labels are not dementia-severity stages. The model uses only official ImageNet initialization and the existing frozen Besga training/validation images. Neither stage checkpoints nor their test outcomes initialize or select this model. It is a small research pilot, not a medically ready diagnostic system.

The prepared protocol and patient manifest are the authoritative machine-readable design. Before fitting, `prepare` pins the trainer, unchanged preprocessing source, original clinical manifest, all 387 training/validation slice pixel hashes, and official ImageNet checkpoint. The 43 unique patients retain their existing splits: 32 train (21 AD/11 CN) and 11 validation (7 AD/4 CN). All nine slices from each patient remain together. No old test, OpenNeuro, Harvard or AlzaSet pixels are opened by the training preparation/runner.

Each patient contributes **one unweighted cross-entropy loss** on the mean of their nine two-class slice logits. At validation/inference, probabilities are `softmax(mean(slice_logits))`. Thus the training objective and patient decision agree. Class weights are explicitly `[1,1]`; no smoothing, calibration fit, or threshold search is performed. AD is predicted when `P_AD >= 0.5`. With these small case-control samples, scores must not be presented as validated clinical disease risks.

The MobileNetV3-Large ImageNet V1 feature extractor feeds a dropout 0.2 / linear 960-to-2 slice head. The first five epochs train only this head; subsequent epochs also train the final three feature blocks. All BatchNorm affine parameters and running statistics stay frozen. AdamW uses head LR1e-4, final-block LR1e-5, weight decay0.001 and gradient clipping5, with fixed cosine decay to5% of the initial rate over100epochs. Each epoch visits every training patient once in shuffled patient batches of4.

Augmentation is conservative and deterministic from seed/epoch/patient: shared rotation±5° with an expanded canvas before resizing (preserves full original FOV), brightness0.92–1.08, contrast0.9–1.1, Gaussian blur0–0.5px with probability0.25, and grayscale Gaussian noise0–0.01 with probability0.5. Noise realizations are independent per slice; other parameters are shared within a patient. There are no flips, crops, translations or cutouts. Evaluation uses the existing224×224RGB pixels and ImageNet normalization exactly.

All three fixed seeds20261021/22/23 follow the same maximum100/minimum20/patience15 rule. Full clean validation patient NLL selects each checkpoint only when it improves by more than1e-4; epoch0 is an eligible fallback and ties retain the earlier checkpoint. A plateau may stop only after20epochs. A maximum-epoch or safety-wall stop explicitly requires validation-only convergence review before external inference. The12-hour suite bound is a failsafe, not evidence of convergence. The user's current machine-price cap is$10/hour without a total-spend cap; root owns all infrastructure and cost decisions.

The current verifier deliberately has **no approval bypass** for a maximum-epoch or safety-wall stop. If a run reaches100epochs while still improving, root must inspect only the complete training/validation histories. The resolution is a separate versioned continuation protocol, frozen before any test prediction, stating the reason, all starting checkpoint/optimizer identities, the same continuation rules for all seeds, learning rates, further ceiling and stopping criterion. Preserve the original run and its unconverged status. A corresponding independently reviewed verifier must check the combined history under that new protocol. A prose declaration of convergence or a renamed completion flag cannot unlock this version's held-out evaluator. A safety failure similarly requires a documented recovery/continuation, never silent partial acceptance.

Save and show every seed, raw augmented-train loss/accuracy, clean-train resubstitution loss/accuracy, and clean-validation loss/accuracy. A small training loss or smooth curve cannot establish external generalization. No seed or epoch may be chosen using medical-test outcomes or Grad-CAM appearance.

The primary new model is a prespecified equal-probability ensemble of all three validation-selected seeds. Individual seed outcomes remain visible. The lowest-validation-NLL seed (then seed ascending) is only the representative for attribution. Independent seeds are not independent patients. The earlier clinical auxiliary models retain their original **mean of slice probabilities**, so comparisons are between complete model pipelines with different training and pooling rules, not an isolated causal test of architecture.

The fresh primary evaluation candidate is the verified Harvard Dataverse release10.7910/DVN/29352: **19 eligible T1 patients (9 AD/10 CN)**. The paper's30people/30functional scans do not imply30releasedT1volumes; there are29T1volumes and10MCI cases remain outside binary scoring. Unchanged nine-slice preprocessing and diagnosis/prediction-blinded gross QC, checksum and cross-source duplicate checks must finish before any prediction. Prior Besga test11 and OpenNeuro results are reused descriptive comparisons. AlzaSet remains quarantined because subject identities/series cannot be validated. No new cohort can affect training, preprocessing parameters, checkpoint, ensemble, threshold or calibration after outcomes are known. Root owns the separately frozen evaluation script and patient-level intervals/paired contrasts.

Limitations include tiny development/validation cohorts, broad uncertainty (especially9ADexternal patients), single-source development, differences in scanner/field strength/population and demographic imbalance, heuristic rather than validated brain preprocessing, lack of biological confirmation for all diagnoses, and absence of prospective workflow or clinical decision-impact validation. More epochs, slices or synthetic distortions do not resolve these limitations.

## Interface for the separately owned evaluator

`build_model()` returns a slice model `[N,3,224,224] -> [N,2]`; it performs no network download. Load `checkpoint['model']` strictly. `build_model(weights_file, initialize_imagenet=True)` is for training only. Checkpoint fields are `model`, `epoch`, `metrics`, `protocol_sha256`, `manifest_sha256`, `seed`, `architecture`, and `selection_metric`; `last.pt` also contains optimizer state.

`patient_rows_from_slices(path, root, cohorts=None, splits=None, verify_pixels=True)` groups the supplied clinical manifest by its documented `group_key`, verifies exactly nine fractions and labels, and returns patient dictionaries. Filters apply before image reads. Each record has `paths` (nine repo-relative paths), `pixel_sha256`, `cohort`, `subject_id`, `group_key`, `diagnosis`, `label`, `split`, and `n_slices`. MCI label is-1 and is excluded from binary metrics.

`preprocess_bag(row, root, train=False, seed=0, epoch=0)` returns `[9,3,224,224]`. `infer_patients(model, rows, root, device, batch_size=4, workers=0)` returns `(metrics, records)`; each record includes the identifiers, `label`, `probabilities` (CN,AD), and `n_slices`. `PatientBags` is the matching DataLoader dataset. The evaluator must assert checkpoint/manifest/source pins and complete membership before scoring.

`verify_completed_suite(suite, prepared, root)` performs no model inference. It refuses incomplete/unconverged suites, checks the full three-seed identities and pins, recomputes NLL from every epoch's exact11validation-patient predictions, replays checkpoint selection and patience, and checks selected checkpoint tensors/metadata. It returns a dictionary with `selections` (absolute checkpoint paths, SHA256s, epochs, validationNLL), `representative_seed`, and protocol/manifest/selection hashes. Root freezes this result before making fresh-cohort predictions.

Outputs per seed: `clinical_seed<seed>/best.pt`, `last.pt`, `selected_validation.json`, `selected_train_clean.json`, every `epochNNN_validation.json`, `history.json/csv`, `raw_patient_curves.png/pdf`, `selection.json`, and `run_status.json`. At suite completion, `validation_selection_before_any_clinical_test.json` identifies all three best checkpoint hashes, exact epochs and validation scores, the fixed ensemble, and representative seed; `suite_status.json` pins that selection file and prepared protocol/manifest. Any incomplete or unconverged status must be resolved before evaluation.

# Figure and caption guidance

This is a drafting aid for the full, unchanged figure collection. Do not remove negative results, pick a model using test performance, crop warning labels, smooth loss histories or choose a Grad-CAM because it looks more anatomical. Individual PDFs are publication-layout assets, not a claim that the experiment has passed clinical validation. Confirm the target journal's format and image-use permissions.

| Figure family | Suggested caption language and necessary qualifications |
|---|---|
| Clinical clean confusion matrices and exact intervals | “Prespecified fixed old and new clinical ensembles on identical patients at AD threshold 0.5. Harvard was source-held-out at Round 2 evaluation; Besga and OpenNeuro were reused cohorts. Denominators are patients, with MCI excluded from binary scoring. Intervals are exact two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson.” Show all three sources separately. Harvard all-AD decisions and OpenNeuro all-CN decisions must remain visible |
| Clinical ROC/PR | “Ranking diagnostics in small convenience case-control cohorts; threshold-independent discrimination does not establish a useful fixed-threshold operating point.” Include 9 AD/10 CN Harvard, 7/4 Besga and 1/14 OpenNeuro. Do not interpret the one-AD OpenNeuro curve as precise population evidence |
| Clinical paired comparison | “New-minus-old differences from 10,000 paired diagnosis-stratified patient bootstrap replicates, conditional on the observed cohorts. Exploratory, unadjusted intervals; not confirmatory clinical-superiority evidence.” Do not rename accuracy differences as balanced-accuracy differences |
| Confidence and reliability | “Uncalibrated model-score diagnostics in case-control samples.” Softmax is not a validated probability of disease. Sparse bins, high confidence or a lower Brier score do not prove clinical calibration |
| Raw learning curves | Identify seed, selected epoch and whether each curve is augmented optimization, clean training, clean validation or stress-selection loss. Histories are unsmoothed. Training losses and validation losses may differ in weighting, augmentation and aggregation; clinical generalization must be evaluated separately |
| Stage accuracy, confusion, ROC/PR | “Reused four-class image benchmark using supplied dataset labels; patient independence is unverified.” State each denominator and keep the 1,058 primary and 756 sensitivity subset distinct. Do not label the supplied classes as clinically adjudicated CDR severity |
| Original100 model/reader comparison | “Historical case-aligned comparison on 100 supplied-label images with three fixed readers. Cases overlap 66 old-training, nine old-validation and 25 old-test rows. Reader range is descriptive, not a confidence interval. This does not establish clinical superiority over neuroradiologists.” The separate binary clinical model is not scored by these four-class labels |
| Pixel stress/region masking | State transformation strength, cohort and model. “Bounded input-sensitivity diagnostics, not validation of scanner, acquisition, motion or clinical-workflow robustness.” On the 160-image validation controls, corner/border erasures retained 160/160 representative accuracy, while corner-only inputs scored 25%; this does not rule out all shortcuts |
| Native attribution location summary | “Native 7×7 Grad-CAM mass for the original classifier, Round 1 c3 and validation-selected Round 2 stage model. Interpolation is for display only, with broad receptive fields; mass is not anatomical probability.” Include the persistent replay-failure statement below |
| Parameter randomization | “Three fixed randomization seeds across head, head-plus-final-block and full network on eight fixed legacy examples; all 72 records retained.” A failed or flat randomized map remains part of the record. Randomization is a diagnostic control, not pathological localization validation |
| The 115 case panels | “Fixed historical stage-model cases; each model's unmodified predicted class is the Grad-CAM target, with differing targets labeled. No anatomical cropping or heatmap relocation.” The complete roster is 100 original cases + eight class-balanced historical examples + seven earlier gallery examples. These are not binary clinical-model maps |
| Round 3 workflow diagrams or documentation | Label “planned research workflow / software-verified only.” There are no Round 3 real-patient learning curves, performance graphs or explanation maps. Synthetic test figures are not clinical data and should not be presented as study results |

Use this warning on every attribution figure or in an unavoidable adjacent caption:

> Diagnostic attribution only. Probability replay exceeded the frozen 0.0001 maximum absolute tolerance in 2/100 original100 cases (0.0001177192 and 0.0003736615); predicted classes agreed. The tolerance was not widened and the failure remains unresolved. These maps do not establish anatomical localization or clinical validity.

For a compact manuscript, use the clinical external-result figures in the main Results, raw curves and full seed/scenario tables in supplements, and the complete warning-bearing attribution collection as a diagnostic appendix. Preserve the full evidence package for coauthor review. A “good Grad-CAM” means legible, mechanically documented and honestly labeled; it does not mean selecting only favorable heatmaps.

The 49-page combined Round 2 atlas was rendered and matched to its reviewed source PDFs. Its two attribution-summary pages remain diagnostic. The 115 case panels are a separate collection, not 115 additional independently sampled patients and not part of the atlas page count.

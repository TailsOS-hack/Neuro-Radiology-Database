# Independent review of the proposed clinical experiment

The design is coherent as a small patient-level AD/control research pilot. It tests whether a dedicated clinical endpoint and subject-level optimization improve over the earlier multitask clinical heads. It does not establish clinical readiness, general MRI robustness, or superiority to neuroradiologists. This review performs no model fitting or inference and does not change the frozen stage-model round2 runtime.

The planned development set stays at 32 Besga training participants (21 AD, 11 control) and 11 validation participants (7 AD, 4 control). Besga's 11 test participants and OpenNeuro's 15 binary-evaluable participants are already-used benchmarks. The newly acquired Dataverse T1 inventory is 29 participants: 9 AD, 10 controls, and 10 MCI. One of the 10 published AD participants has no available structural T1, documented before inference. Thus the new binary external denominator is **19**, not 20 or 30; the 10 MCI participants have no binary accuracy denominator. Acquisition counts are reported by root and require the final provider-derived manifest and integrity record in the experiment freeze.

The Dataverse source includes anatomical T1 MDEFT scans, although the dataset title emphasizes resting-state fMRI. The published description reports a 3T Siemens acquisition, while the Besga descriptor reports a 1.5T scanner. The former publication also reports demographic differences between the full published AD and control groups, including mean ages 72.3 versus 66.0 and differing sex and education composition. Those published full-cohort summaries cannot be silently substituted for demographic summaries of the 29 available T1 participants. Sources: [Dataverse dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/29352), [published acquisition/demographic description](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0231294), [Besga original data descriptor](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnagi.2020.583212/full).

1. **Freeze the patient endpoint and aggregation.** Use the same `softmax(mean_of_nine_slice_logits)` for the patient training loss, validation NLL, and new-model inference. Each patient supplies one loss contribution per epoch, not nine nominally independent examples. The fixed 9 slices are a heuristic T1 representation, not a comprehensive 3D or anatomically registered assessment. Keep all splits and any repeated acquisitions grouped by true provider subject identity. Dataverse subject numbers repeat across diagnostic folders, so provider category/archive hierarchy must be part of the canonical source identity; filename suffixes alone are not a label join. Check raw-file, canonical-volume and processed-pixel duplicates across all source cohorts; these checks can find overlap but do not prove cross-repository patient independence. AlzaSet stays quarantined until a defensible image-to-patient mapping exists.

2. **Keep preprocessing uniform and gross QC independent of predictions.** Reuse the existing deterministic RAS/1.5mm pipeline, fixed 45–85% cranial-span fractions, per-volume percentiles, aspect-preserving padding and 224-pixel transform. That heuristic does not perform validated skull stripping, bias correction or atlas registration. Verify 3D T1 input, affine handedness/orientation, voxel spacing, finite intensities, complete field of view, plausible cranial span, nine distinct slices and consistent per-subject metadata. Inspect all 29 external central slices and fixed, identity-based full-bag samples with diagnosis/prediction hidden. Prespecify fatal failures and document every exclusion with its original denominator. A failed input must stop or enter a declared missing/excluded category; it cannot be replaced by another scan after seeing predictions. Any change to preprocessing motivated by the external cohort makes it an adapted external cohort and must be disclosed; a failed preprocessing experiment is preferable to silently optimizing this test set. No normalization, segmentation fitting, harmonization, thresholds or cropping parameters may be fitted on external participants. Expanded-canvas small rotations preserve the field of view; noise/intensity/blur parameters remain fixed and mild, with no anatomical crop or flip.

3. **Keep the small development experiment bounded.** ImageNet initialization, frozen BatchNorm statistics/affine parameters, a short head-only phase, and limited final-block tuning are reasonable low-data choices. They do not remove overfitting risk. The training agent accepted unweighted patient cross-entropy, matching unweighted validation NLL, rather than a class-weighted probability objective. Record the initialization checksum, frozen/trainable blocks, optimizer, augmentation, patience, min_delta and all three seeds before fitting. Select checkpoints only by the fixed patient validation NLL, including the declared epoch0 fallback. Show raw augmented-training, clean-training and validation loss/accuracy separately. Do not enlarge the search in response to poor external results. Eleven validation patients give a noisy stopping signal: one error changes accuracy by 9.1 percentage points, AD sensitivity by 14.3 points and control specificity by 25 points. Three seeds measure optimization variability, not uncertainty from alternate patient partitions.

4. **Preserve comparator and calibration definitions.** Keep c2/c4/c6 frozen checkpoints, original image transform and original subject aggregation by mean slice probability. A new mean-logit, dedicated-head system differs in task, optimization, head and pooling; its comparison is a whole-pipeline comparison, not an isolated ablation of any one feature. New seeds 20261021/22/23 are not matched to the old 20261011/12/13 seeds. If the uniform three-seed probability ensemble is the proposed primary model, freeze that rule now, retain every constituent, and never promote a favorable seed after testing. Fix the class order, positive label AD and the 0.5 threshold/tie rule before inference. Report NLL and binary Brier with patient-level confidence plots; sparse ECE bins are descriptive only. Do not fit temperature scaling, an isotonic calibrator or a threshold on 11 validation patients or on the external cohort. The case-control sampling proportions are not disease prevalence, so predicted scores, PPV/NPV and apparent calibration do not establish population risk calibration.

5. **Use patient-level, cohort-specific inference and conservative conclusions.** Report AD sensitivity, control specificity, balanced accuracy, accuracy, macroF1, ROC/AUC, confusion counts, NLL and Brier for each reused and fresh cohort separately. Do not pool the 19 external patients across three seeds into 57 observations. Show every seed plus the prespecified ensemble; report optimization spread separately from patient uncertainty. Use two-sided exact binomial intervals for accuracy/sensitivity/specificity. Any paired old/new bootstrap must resample the same patients for all models; class-stratified paired bootstrap for balanced metrics/AUC is a defensible prespecified option, with the conditioning stated. Include paired discordance counts; avoid many unadjusted superiority tests. AUC has only 9 AD and 10 control participants, not 90 independent pair observations. As illustrations of sample-size limitations, even a hypothetical 19/19 correct result has a 95% exact accuracy interval of 82.4–100%; hypothetical 9/9 AD detection has an interval of 66.4–100%. These are examples, not observed results. OpenNeuro's sole AD case cannot support stable external sensitivity. MCI scores remain descriptive and cannot be labeled early-AD detection, progression prediction, or accuracy. The original 100 neuroradiologist comparison uses a different legacy severity endpoint and cannot validate this binary clinical model.

The highest-value safeguards are the untouched prediction gate for Dataverse, patient-level aggregation and denominator checks, unchanged preprocessing with documented gross QC, and complete reporting of every fixed comparator. Additional epochs or better-looking curves cannot compensate for 32 training patients, one small new external source, unresolved demographic/source confounding, or the absence of prospective clinical evaluation.

Review status: methodological review complete; final protocol/manifest hashes, source-level overlap audit, preprocessing QC and implementation tests remain prerequisites to fitting/evaluating this separate experiment. No training or inference was performed by this review.
